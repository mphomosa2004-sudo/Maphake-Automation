About Us

BUILT UNDER A
FAMILY NAME

A Johannesburg digital agency on a mission to give every South African business access to world-class digital tools.

Maphake Automation is a Johannesburg-based digital agency founded on one belief: every South African business deserves world-class digital tools — regardless of size or industry.

We carry the Maphake name with pride. It's a name tied to a legacy of entrepreneurship and a commitment to building things that last. Every website, every chatbot, and every automation we deliver reflects that standard.

We serve businesses across all industries — from home-based salons and independent contractors to established companies looking to modernise. No client is too small. No project is too ambitious.
